Contact support

Email REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL. Include your Player ID, app version, device model and a description. Never send your password or full payment-card details.

Purchase pending or coins missing

  1. Do not buy the same pack again.
  2. Confirm the Apple purchase completed.
  3. Sign in to the same game account.
  4. Open Coin Shop and select Sync Purchases.
  5. If coins remain missing, keep the Apple transaction receipt and contact support.

Refunds

Apple processes billing refunds. Use Apple’s “Report a Problem” service for a payment refund. Publisher support can investigate delivery, account and wallet synchronization but cannot access your complete card details.

Delete account and data

Open Profile → Settings → Delete Account & Data, enter the current password and type DELETE. This removes gameplay profile data. Required purchase, fraud, tax, dispute or legal records may remain for the period stated in the Privacy Policy.

Safer play

Golden Wilds is simulated gambling with no cash-out. Purchases do not improve odds. Take regular breaks and stop if play is uncomfortable. Parents and guardians can restrict purchases through Apple Screen Time and Ask to Buy.
