# F34 guardian anatomy v3 — superseded

The user rejected the 2026-10-08 v3 construction. Its limb counts were insufficient to establish natural anatomy: the lion combined an older waving foreleg with a repainted torso, and the swan’s pearl-carrying wing looked like an extra arm. The shoulder connection and pose remained unsuitable.

The current replacement is [complete coherent guardians, 2026-10-09](guardian-coherent-20261009.md). Both figures were newly commissioned as complete poses, with animation parts extracted from their own painting. V3 is no longer installed by either preparation entry point.

The [v3 screenshots and recordings](screenshots/f34-corrected-anatomy-v3-20261008/) are retained only as rejected historical evidence. The six earlier runtime cases checked animation mechanics and browser errors; their success did not validate the naturalness of that art or establish acceptance.
