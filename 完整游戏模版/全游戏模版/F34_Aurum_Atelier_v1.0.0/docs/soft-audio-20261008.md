# F34 rounded festive audio — 2026-10-08

Request: keep the soundtrack cheerful, festive and rich in wealth character, with less piercing sound.

## Change

Regenerated all 44 stereo audio assets: the salon and four cabinet backgrounds, seven feedback cues, and counting / resolution music for all four jackpot tiers in all four themes. Melodies, BPM, clip lengths, resource names and UUIDs remain stable. Click and reel-stop notes use a lower register; upper-octave glockenspiel accents in larger awards were lowered.

Reduced bell and brass upper harmonics, softened instrument / percussion attacks, and applied broad offline mastering: a 2.6 kHz shelf at −4.5 dB, a broad 2.3 kHz presence dip at −1.5 dB and a smooth rolloff above 8.5 kHz. Saturation drive is lower. These settings are recorded in `art-source/audio-manifest.json` and generated by `art-source/compose_audio.py`; they apply to packaged audio on every target.

Feedback playback levels are lower, with clicks at 0.36, reel stops at 0.50, award counting at 0.62 and resolution at 0.60. The background level remains 0.42. Existing fades, music ducking, mute controls and background lifecycle handling are preserved.

## Verification

- All 44 WAVs retain stereo / 32 kHz / 16-bit format and duration. UUIDs are unchanged; six loops have matching endpoints. No asset clips; the maximum sample peak is 0.6500.
- After normalizing each clip to its total spectral energy, the five backgrounds show median changes of -3.97 dB in the 2–5 kHz band and -7.17 dB above 5 kHz. Jackpot counting / resolution clips show median changes of -3.46 dB and -5.44 dB, respectively. This verifies a timbre change independent of overall volume; it is not a subjective listening score.
- TypeScript typecheck passed. Creator 3.8.8 reported completed web-mobile output. Its CLI returned 36; completion logs and all 44 built WAV hashes were independently checked against the current source assets.
- Seven runtime cases passed: BIG in all four themes, plus SUPER, MEGA and EPIC. They cover actual WebAudio output, mute / unmute, background hide / show, reel audio, counting / resolution, award exit, source cleanup, balance settlement and salon music recovery. Fixtures exist only in isolated test contexts.
- The first paused-meter attempt retained a nonzero analyser buffer although every managed source was paused. A zero-valued source now keeps the isolated meter graph processing; the unchanged pause logic then passed both source-state and output-silence checks. This test-only source does not enter the game mix.
- The continuous Pearl BIG recording captures the Cocos canvas and real managed audio on one clock, including automatic reveal / cleanup and return to the salon. No browser errors were reported. Reduced motion was not rerun for this audio-only change.
