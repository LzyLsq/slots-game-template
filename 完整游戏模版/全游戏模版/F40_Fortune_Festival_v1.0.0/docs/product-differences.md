# F40 · Fortune Festival / 万福金街

New original lantern festival on a riverside merchant street. Wealth comes from craftsmanship, precious jade, coins and celebration. No F36 images are used. Reliable authentication, wallet, loading and settlement infrastructure are reused.

| Area | F36 | F40 |
|---|---|---|
| World | Wildlife expedition | Riverside lantern festival, four fortune houses |
| Hall | Four sanctuary tiles | One featured architectural gate, four house selectors, festival album |
| Golden Phoenix | 20 lines with sticky free spins | 10 lines, five sticky free spins |
| Jade Dragon | Cash collector | Variable ways: 2–4 active rows on five reels; hold treasures |
| Pearl Qilin | Connected cascades | Count-anywhere groups on 6×4 board with escalating celebration drops |
| Amber Panda | One centre line | Two 3×3 boards: five lines per board, total stake split equally |
| Progress | Fixed ten-spin seals | Four daily festival stamps (five settled paid spins each) and daily parade completion reward |
| Visuals | Wildlife temples | Lacquer, gold, jade, pearl, lantern architecture, cartoon lucky merchants integrated above crowns |
| Audio | Wildlife sanctuaries | Warm plucked festival orchestra, soft drums, celebratory gold and chime accents |

Coins are virtual. Auth/payment availability stays explicit. Distinction is an implementation goal, not a guarantee of App Store approval.
