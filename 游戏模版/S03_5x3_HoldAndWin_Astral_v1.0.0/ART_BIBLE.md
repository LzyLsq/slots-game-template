# Astral Vault Art Bible

- Direction: premium celestial observatory treasury, stylized 3D commercial mobile-game rendering.
- Palette: space navy `#05091D`, royal violet `#342064`, starcore cyan `#25D7FF`, molten gold `#E3A63B`, pearl `#F4F0E8`.
- Composition: the reels remain the largest focal point; title, balance, jackpots and controls use distinct non-overlapping vertical bands in portrait and a two-column layout in landscape.
- Metal: engraved gold alloy with broad bevels and limited hot highlights; never flat yellow.
- Crystal: large readable facets, one inner light source and strong 100 px silhouettes.
- UI: generated title crest, jackpot beam, reel cabinet, starcore spin device, locked-coin socket, feature crown and utility bezel.
- Character: the masked Vault Keeper appears only in a dedicated framed landscape display so the source image never covers reels or controls.
- VFX: cyan shockwaves and physical gold coin motion use controlled opacity; no full-screen white flashing.
- Typography: Lilita One for short display words and Fredoka for values and rules; runtime text is never baked into generated art.
