# Paytable

Awards below are multipliers of the per-line bet for 3 / 4 / 5 matching symbols:

- Amethyst: 6 / 15 / 45
- Ruby: 6 / 18 / 54
- Sapphire: 9 / 24 / 72
- Emerald: 9 / 30 / 90
- Topaz: 12 / 36 / 120
- Moonstone: 15 / 48 / 150
- Scepter: 18 / 66 / 210
- Astrolabe: 24 / 84 / 270
- Crown: 30 / 120 / 420
- Chalice: 36 / 150 / 540
- Wild: 45 / 210 / 750

Three or more Scatters pay total-bet multiples. Regular Hold & Win coins award 1×, 2×, 3×, 5× or 10× total bet. Jackpot coins award MINI 10×, MINOR 25×, MAJOR 100× or GRAND 500×. Filling all 15 positions awards an additional 500× GRAND.
