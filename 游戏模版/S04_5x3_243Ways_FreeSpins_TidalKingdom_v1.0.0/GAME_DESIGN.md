# Game Design

Tidal Kingdom is the fourth distinct template in the collection. It is a 5×3 243-Ways game with a progressive free-spin multiplier, not a center-line game, cluster cascade or Hold & Win reskin.

## Base game

Each wager creates a five-reel result. A regular symbol wins when it appears on reel 1 and continues across at least three adjacent reels. Matching row counts on each reel multiply together, so a 3×3×3×3×3 pattern produces all 243 ways. WILD substitutes for regular symbols; SCATTER is evaluated anywhere.

## Free spins

Three, four or five PORTAL SCATTERS award 8, 12 or 15 free spins. The feature has its own state controller, remaining-spin display and TIDE meter. It starts at 1×; after each winning free spin the multiplier rises by one and persists, capped at 5×. Three or more SCATTERS in the feature retrigger three spins.

## Presentation and controls

Outcome generation is separated from animation, so quick stop and Turbo change presentation timing only. Auto performs up to ten real wagers and stops when requested or when credits are insufficient. Sound, HOME, recharge, bet controls, symbol inspection, Rules and the no-credit-change Free-Spin Demo all have visible press feedback and real actions.

The interface is built around the 243-Ways mechanic instead of reusing the Hold & Win console. Five visually separated reel channels feed a live `3 → 9 → 27 → 81 → 243` ways path as the reels land. The TIDE meter is attached to the reel cabinet and reflects the real free-spin multiplier. The primary SPIN control is centered; wager and Auto controls have separate left/right roles, while secondary utilities sit on their own lower rail.

Portrait uses this reel-centric vertical composition. Landscape/desktop rearranges the same functional groups into a two-zone layout without reverting to the S03 jackpot-header or right-hand SPIN structure.
